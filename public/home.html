03 · EB1A Support

Extraordinary ability documentation.

Comprehensive documentation support for the EB-1A extraordinary ability visa — evidence strategy, narrative framing, and complete material preparation for a petition that stands out.

What we build for you

The ten criteria, built strong.

USCIS evaluates EB-1A petitions against 10 criteria. We help you identify, document, and present the strongest possible evidence for each one that applies to you.

Awards & Prizes
Identifying, documenting, and framing nationally or internationally recognised awards in your field.
Membership in Associations
Documenting membership in organisations that require outstanding achievement for entry.
Press & Media Coverage
Compiling published material about you and your work in professional or major trade publications.
Judging the Work of Others
Documenting peer review, conference programme committees, editorial boards, and competitions.
Original Contributions
Building the evidence package for major, original contributions of major significance in the field.
Scholarly Articles
Citation mapping, impact documentation, and venue analysis for your publication record.
Critical Role
Documenting a leading or critical role in distinguished organisations or establishments.
High Remuneration
Salary benchmarking and evidence packaging to demonstrate commanding remuneration.
What we produce

The full package.

Petitioner cover letter & narrative
A compelling, attorney-ready narrative that ties your evidence to the extraordinary ability standard.
Expert reference letters
Drafting strong, specific expert letters from referees who know your work — framed for USCIS criteria.
Evidence compilation & indexing
Organising, labelling, and cross-referencing every exhibit for a clean, reviewable package.
Field impact documentation
Citation analysis, h-index framing, and comparison to peers to demonstrate scope of influence.
Important note
We are writers, not lawyers

We produce documentation, narrative, and evidence packages. We work alongside immigration attorneys — we do not provide legal advice. The combination of strong writing and expert legal counsel gives petitions the best chance.

A strong EB-1A petition is built over months, not days. Come to us early — before you need to file.

Our process

How the book gets written.

01
Voice & vision session
We interview you deeply — understanding your thinking, your audience, your tone, and the argument at the heart of the book. This session becomes the brief.
02
Outline & structure
We draft a full outline — chapters, sections, argument flow — and review it with you until it matches your vision before a word of body text is written.

Research & first draft
We research deeply, write in your voice, and produce a full first draft chapter by chapter — with your input at each stage, not just the end.

Review & revision
You read, mark up, and we revise until the book sounds exactly like you — not like it was written for you by someone else.
05
Final delivery
Publication-ready manuscript, formatted for your target platform — Kindle, PDF distribution, print-on-demand, or proprietary distribution.
